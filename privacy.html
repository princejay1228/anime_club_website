Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information this website handles. The short version: browsing the site collects nothing about you. If you sign in to the Member Lounge, we keep only what is needed to run club polls and event comments.

1. Browsing the website

Visiting the website does not require an account. There is no advertising or analytics tracking. The site remembers a few choices in your own browser (for example whether you have seen the intro and whether music is on); these never leave your device.

2. The Member Lounge (sign-in, polls and comments)

If you choose to sign in to the Member Lounge with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We then ask for your full name and, optionally, your year and department, so the club admins can recognise and approve you. We also store:

  • your poll votes (results are shown only as totals; other members never see who voted for what);
  • your comments on events, which are shown with your name and photo once approved;
  • your membership status, and any admin decisions about your account (approval, suspension and the reason given);
  • if you hold an office-bearer title (President, Vice President, Secretary or Treasurer), that title, which is shown publicly with your name and photo;
  • when you last opened the club notices, so the bell can show what is new.

Club admins (office bearers chosen by the Club) can see your name, email, year, department and comments, and use them only to run the Club. Deleted or removed comments are kept for a short time so admins can review moderation decisions.

3. Where it is stored

Member Lounge data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. When you ask to join, the club's admin email account receives a notification with your name, email, year and department, sent through Resend. Club events are read from a Google Sheet maintained by the Club, which contains no member information.

Approved members receive club notices and meeting cancellations by email, sent from the Club's Gmail account (animeclubsimats@gmail.com) through Google. Members are blind-copied, so no one sees anyone else's address. If you would rather not get these emails, tell an office bearer or write to animeclubsimats@gmail.com; the notices stay readable under the bell in the Member Lounge.

Photos and videos in the "Recent meets" gallery are kept in the Club's Google Drive and shown from Google's servers. If you appear in a photo and would like it removed, write to animeclubsimats@gmail.com or tell an office bearer.

4. Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. This is handled under Vercel's privacy policy.

5. Your choices

You can browse everything without signing in. You can delete your own comments at any time. To have your Member Lounge account and its data deleted, email animeclubsimats@gmail.com or ask an office bearer; we will remove it within 30 days. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

6. Contacting the Club

If you contact the Club through a link on this site (for example email or social media), the information you share is used only to reply to you and organise Club activities.

7. Photos and member information

Names and photos of office bearers are shown with their consent. Any member can ask for their name or photo to be removed from the website by contacting an office bearer.

8. Children

This website is intended for college students and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

This policy is a general template for a student club. Review it with the faculty coordinator so it matches SIMATS policy.
