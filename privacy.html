Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information this website handles. The short version: the website itself does not collect your personal information.

1. What we collect

The website has no sign-up forms, accounts, comment boxes, advertising or analytics trackers, and it sets no cookies of its own. Fonts and images are served from the website itself, not from third-party services.

2. Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. This is handled under Vercel's privacy policy.

3. Contacting the Club

If you contact the Club through a link on this site (for example email or social media), the information you share is used only to reply to you and organise Club activities, and is not sold or shared for marketing.

4. Photos and member information

Names and photos of office bearers are shown with their consent. Any member can ask for their name or photo to be removed from the website by contacting an office bearer.

5. Children

This website is intended for college students and is not directed at children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

If the Club later adds forms, analytics or event registration, this policy must be updated before those features go live.
