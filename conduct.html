Code of Conduct

Last updated: 30 October 2026

SIMATS Anime Club is a friendly space for everyone who enjoys anime, manga and Japanese pop culture. These guidelines apply at Club events, in Club online groups and wherever you represent the Club.

Be kind and inclusive

  • Welcome newcomers, whatever shows they like and however much they have watched.
  • No harassment, bullying, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
  • Debate shows and characters, not people.

Respect at events

  • Follow the instructions of office bearers and SIMATS rules on campus.
  • Keep content shown at Club events suitable for a mixed college audience.
  • No spoilers without a clear warning.

Cosplay and photos

  • Cosplay is not consent. Always ask before photographing or touching anyone, and respect a "no".
  • Costumes and props must follow campus safety rules; no real or realistic weapons.
  • Ask before posting photos of other members online.

Respect creators

  • Support official releases where possible.
  • Credit artists when sharing fan art, and never present someone else's work as your own.

Reporting a problem

If something makes you uncomfortable, speak to any office bearer: President Sudharshshini R, Vice President Darshan A, Secretary Jayadeep J or Treasurer Lohithaksha D. Reports are handled privately. Breaking this code can lead to removal from Club activities.
