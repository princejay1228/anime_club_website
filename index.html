THE CLUB

WHAT WE DO

Four ways to be part of SIMATS Anime Club. Come for one, stay for all of them.

上映

Screenings

Watch parties for new seasons, classic films and community-voted picks, with discussion after.

仮装

Cosplay

Costume workshops, prop-making help and photo walks. Beginners and pros are equally welcome.

絵

Art Jams

Sketch sessions, fan-art challenges and manga panel practice. Bring a pencil or a tablet.

祭

Quizzes & Fests

Spin-the-wheel quizzes, trivia battles and stalls at college fests, with prizes for the winners.

CALENDAR

UPCOMING EVENTS

We meet every Tuesday. Holidays are marked, so you always know when we’re on.
